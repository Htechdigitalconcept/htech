How I got here.

Over the past six years I have designed and developed more than 30 websites across different industries and business types, for clients locally and internationally. That range has shaped a practical approach: understand the business first, design for the people who will use the site, then build it so it can be maintained with confidence.

I design websites, build them in WordPress and WooCommerce, and go further when a project needs it. Using AI-assisted “vibe coding” alongside my own code in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python, I build custom web apps, chatbots, Telegram bots and automations that connect to the site. One person from design to deployment, so nothing gets lost between teams.

Design philosophy

What I believe a good website should do.

A clear set of principles keeps the decisions consistent from the first sketch to the final launch.

Visual communication

Typography, spacing and imagery work together to explain the business before a visitor reads a single line.

Structure before decoration

The right pages, in the right order, with a journey that makes sense to someone arriving for the first time.

User experience

Clear navigation, readable content and obvious next steps remove the friction that loses enquiries.

Responsive by default

Every layout is designed to work on a phone, then adapted thoughtfully for larger screens.

Functionality that supports the goal

Features are included because they help the site do its job, not because they look impressive in a demo.

Conversion opportunities

Calls to action are placed where a visitor is ready to act, with language that says exactly what happens next.
