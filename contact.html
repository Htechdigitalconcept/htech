Frequently asked questions

A few answers before you reach out.

Still have a question? Email me directly and I’ll point you in the right direction.

What should I include in my first message?

A short description of your business, what you need the website to do, any pages or features you have in mind, your timeline and links to examples you like. If you already have a site, include its URL.

What kinds of website projects do you take on?

I work on WordPress business websites, Elementor builds, landing pages, WooCommerce stores, redesigns and improvements to existing sites. I also build custom web apps, AI chatbots, Telegram bots and automations. Share your goals and I can let you know if I’m a good fit.

How much does a website project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, number of pages, features and content requirements. Send me a brief and I can review what you need before discussing a suitable estimate.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. I work with local and international clients. We can discuss the project remotely through email, Upwork or LinkedIn.

Can you improve or redesign my current website?

Yes. I can review an existing site and discuss improvements to its layout, mobile experience, content structure, performance or WordPress setup. Include the website link in your message.

What happens after I contact you?

I’ll review the information you send, follow up if I need clarification and suggest a sensible next step for the project.
