Project overview

Visitors often have a question before they are ready to fill in a form. This assistant answers common questions straight away and adds practical guidance based on the visitor’s business. When someone needs a person, their message comes to me on Telegram and my reply appears back in the same chat.

Features

What the chatbot does.

Built to help a visitor quickly and to keep me in the conversation when it matters.

Answers from verified facts

Matches questions to a feed of published Htech facts and FAQs, so answers stay accurate. It is not a generative AI model, so it does not make things up.

Guidance for the visitor’s business

Adds practical next steps depending on whether the visitor runs an online store, a service business, an existing site or a new one.

Greets and welcomes

A simple hello gets a friendly welcome message straight away, with no waiting.

Live handoff on Telegram

Visitor messages are sent to me on Telegram. When I use Telegram’s Reply, my answer appears in that visitor’s chat.

One chat per visitor

Each browser tab gets its own random session ID, so replies always reach the right person.

Short-lived, private chats

Sessions and up to 40 owner replies are kept for 24 hours only. There is no permanent transcript, and the widget tells visitors this.

Direct contact shortcuts

Icon shortcuts to WhatsApp, email and LinkedIn. The WhatsApp link can open with a short summary the visitor can edit before sending.

Built with safeguards

Message length limits, request rate limiting, and credentials kept on the server in environment variables, never in browser code.

How it works

One conversation, two sides.

  1. Visitor opens the chat

    The widget starts a private session and shows a welcome message.

  2. Assistant answers

    It matches the question to verified entries and adds guidance for the visitor’s situation.

  3. Message reaches Telegram

    Each message is forwarded to me, tagged with a short session label.

  4. Reply returns to the visitor

    Using Telegram’s Reply sends my answer back to that exact chat.
