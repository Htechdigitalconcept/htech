Project overview

A single-page check misses problems that repeat across a site. The Advanced Site Auditor follows same-site links and the sitemap, inspects up to 12 public pages and turns what it finds into a prioritized list of things to review.

The findings shown in the animation are example content, not a real scan.

Features

What it does.

Same-site crawl, up to 12 pages

Follows internal links and the sitemap, and stays on the site that was entered.

Technical checks

Titles, descriptions, headings, canonical and mobile signals, and duplicates.

Sitemap and robots.txt

Checks sitemap entries and honors basic robots.txt disallow rules.

Redirects and broken URLs

Flags redirect chains and broken URLs it discovers.

Prioritized findings

High and Medium findings, plus an inventory of the pages inspected.

Three exports

A branded PDF, an issues CSV and the full JSON.

Scans safely

Public HTTP and HTTPS pages only, with limits on pages, size and time. It submits no forms and runs no page scripts. Results are not stored.

Automated tests

Tests cover the audit engine and the PDF report.

How it works

From start to result.

  1. Enter a public URL

    Start with a public website address.

  2. Bounded crawl

    The Python backend inspects a limited sample of pages.

  3. Review findings

    See prioritized issues and what was inspected.

  4. Download the report

    Take away a PDF, CSV or JSON.
