Design focus

Designed and developed a modern, professional WordPress website for a therapy and counseling practice. The project focused on creating a calm, trustworthy, and user-friendly digital experience that clearly presents therapy services and guides visitors toward booking a consultation. I implemented responsive web design, custom UI/UX, clear service sections, strategic calls-to-action, mobile optimization, intuitive navigation, and a professional visual system.
