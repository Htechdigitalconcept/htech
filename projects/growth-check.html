Project overview

Most owners do not know where to start with their website. Growth Check begins with one question and shows only what is relevant: a live scan for people who already have a site, or a first-website blueprint for people who do not.

It is free, needs no account and asks for no email, so it gives a useful next step before any conversation about services. The animation shown here is a demo of the flow.

Features

What it does.

Two paths from one question

“I already have a website” or “I don’t have a website yet” decides everything that follows.

Live website scan

Checks the homepage and up to two high-priority links on the same domain, then shows what is working and what to improve.

First-website blueprint

The kind of business shapes the goals and recommendations, then a starter blueprint is built.

Adapts to the visitor

Questions and next steps change with the visitor’s situation and business type.

No sales gate

Free, with no account and no email required.

Private by design

Public pages only. Private pages and login-only areas are not scanned.

Honest about its limits

It reads public HTML signals. It does not sign in, submit forms, run page JavaScript or test every page, and it says so.

Automated tests

Tests cover the scan logic, goal selection and the proposal output.

How it works

From start to result.

  1. Choose a path

    Say whether you already have a website or are planning one.

  2. Share the basics

    Enter a public address, or pick your business type and goals.

  3. Scan or blueprint

    The tool connects, inspects and prioritizes, or builds the blueprint.

  4. Get a next step

    You receive practical, plain-language recommendations.
